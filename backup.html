Backup

Save a copy of everything these apps keep for you into one file, to store wherever you like: a USB stick, another cloud, an email to yourself. Your data never leaves this device unless you move the file.

On this device

Each browser, and on an iPhone each home-screen app, keeps its own data. This page sees what this browser holds. For an app on your iPhone home screen, use Back up my data in that app's About panel.

Your Google Drive copy

Apps you signed in to also keep a synced copy in a private folder of your Google Drive. Adding it puts the data from your other devices into the same file.

The file is not encrypted and can hold personal data (training logs, recordings, notes): keep it somewhere private. Sign-in tokens are left out.

Check a backup file

Open a file you saved earlier to see what is inside. Nothing is changed.

Restore

Putting a backup back into the apps is not built yet. Keep your files: they hold everything needed, and will restore once that arrives.
