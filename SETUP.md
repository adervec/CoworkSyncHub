# CoworkSyncHub: setup

Everything that used to need a folder on your disk, a desktop browser tab kept open, and an agent pointed at
the folder now goes through **your own cloud folder and the AI runner on your computer**:

- **AI cowork** (an app asks an AI, the answer comes back for you to review), **the fitness feed** (Garmin and
  Strava, merged once for every app) and **the daily brief**: [RUNNER.md](RUNNER.md), step by step, with a
  troubleshooting table.
- **Signing in** (your synced copy, on every device): the hub's Settings page, or any app's sync settings.
- **Backups and restores**: the hub's Backup page.

The old folder method's setup and troubleshooting is kept for reference in
[archive/SETUP-folder-method.md](archive/SETUP-folder-method.md).
