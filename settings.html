Hub settings

Your account

Signing in is optional. It lets your hub settings follow you to your other devices, through one small file in a private folder of your Google Drive. Google asks again after an hour or on your next visit: one tap, no password.

Look

Every app has this same list of themes, and each remembers its own choice. This one is the hub's. See them all.

Apps on your hub

Untick an app that is not for you. It leaves the hub's tiles, cards, character sheet and suggestions. Nothing is deleted: its data stays, and it still appears in backups.

Your activity

The apps note what you did (a workout, a reading session) in this browser, and the hub reads it here to draw the sheet. If you sign in, the notes also go to your own private Drive folder, one file per device, so the sheet counts your phone and your computer together. Switched off, nothing is shown and nothing is sent to Drive.

What is stored, and where

  • In this browser: everything each app keeps for you, plus the hub's own notes: which apps you opened and when, which are on your home screen, your activity, your theme and these settings.
  • In your Google Drive, only if you sign in: each app's synced copy, the hub's settings file and your activity notes (one file per device), in a private folder that only these apps can read. It is not among your Drive files.
  • With the maker: nothing. There are no accounts, servers or tracking. The maker cannot see your data.
  • Other services the pages talk to: Google, for sign-in and Drive, when you use them.

Erasing clears the hub's notes in this browser (opens, activity, hidden apps, last backup). It does not touch any app's data or your Drive.
